Why ngu-form

Typed from your model
Every key needs a spec, and type: 'email' on a number is a compile error. Mistakes show up in your editor, not in testing.
Signal Forms underneath
Validation, state and binding are Angular's own. form.tree is a normal field tree, so your tests and tools keep working.
Accessible by default
Labels, descriptions, aria-invalid, focus on the first problem and one polite announcement after a refused submit. The demo is checked with axe.
Plain objects
A spec is data. Fields can come from JSON sent by your server, with their rules and conditions, and the model follows the list.
